# AI Paradigms and Knowledge
> Artificial intelligence has been attempted four ways: write the rules (symbolic), estimate a
> function from data (statistical), learn the representation too (neural), and reason under
> uncertainty over an explicit structure (probabilistic). This sub-area explains each paradigm on
> its own terms, then shows how the strongest 2026 systems combine them.

**Start here:** read [Symbolic AI and Good Old-Fashioned AI](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/symbolic-ai-and-good-old-fashioned-ai/symbolic-ai-and-good-old-fashioned-ai) for the tradition every AI word comes from, then work down the index; finish on the [Neuro-Symbolic AI Overview](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/neuro-symbolic-ai-overview/neuro-symbolic-ai-overview), which is the doorway to the depth track.

## Concept Index
Each page is a resource card: a short guided path plus the best free courses, videos, papers,
articles, and books on that paradigm. Read them in order — each one is a reaction to the last.

### The four paradigms
1. [Symbolic AI and Good Old-Fashioned AI](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/symbolic-ai-and-good-old-fashioned-ai/symbolic-ai-and-good-old-fashioned-ai) — logic, search, planning, expert systems, and the bottleneck that stalled them.
2. [The Statistical Learning Paradigm](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/statistical-learning-paradigm/statistical-learning-paradigm) — learning a function from samples, and the generalization vocabulary that came with it.
3. [The Neural Learning Paradigm](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/neural-learning-paradigm/neural-learning-paradigm) — connectionism and learned representations, from the perceptron to foundation models.
4. [Probabilistic Reasoning and Graphical Models](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/probabilistic-reasoning-and-graphical-models/probabilistic-reasoning-and-graphical-models) — Bayesian networks, Markov random fields, and inference under uncertainty.

### Putting them back together
5. [Neuro-Symbolic AI Overview](/ai-ml/ai-ml-learning-resources/foundations/ai-paradigms-and-knowledge/neuro-symbolic-ai-overview/neuro-symbolic-ai-overview) — the 2024-26 revival: solver-calling agents, knowledge-grounded retrieval, and neural proof search.

## Courses (free)
- [MIT 6.034 Artificial Intelligence (Fall 2010)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-034-artificial-intelligence-fall-2010/) — **MIT OpenCourseWare (Patrick Winston)** — the symbolic tradition taught by the person who wrote its textbook.
- [Stanford CS221: Artificial Intelligence — Principles and Techniques](https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2024/) — **Stanford (Percy Liang, Dorsa Sadigh)** — search, logic, probability, and learning in one syllabus, which is exactly this sub-area's point.

## Videos
- [Stanford CS221: Artificial Intelligence (Autumn 2021)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOca_Ovz1DvdtWuz8BfSWL2) — **Stanford Online** — the full lecture series covering all four paradigms.
- [Neural networks (full series)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi) — **3Blue1Brown** — the visual case for the connectionist paradigm.

## Key Papers
- [Computer Science as Empirical Inquiry: Symbols and Search](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/360018.360022) — **Newell & Simon (1976)** — the symbolic paradigm's founding claim.
- [Deep Learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14539) — **LeCun, Bengio & Hinton (2015)** — the neural paradigm's founding claim, forty years later.
- [Defining neurosymbolic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11127) — **De Smet & De Raedt (2025)** — the current attempt to state precisely what combining them means.

## Articles / Blogs (free, no paywall)
- [The Third AI Summer: AAAI Robert S. Engelmore Memorial Lecture](https://ojs.aaai.org/aimagazine/index.php/aimagazine/article/view/19122) — **Henry Kautz (AI Magazine, 2022)** — the history of the paradigm swings, plus the taxonomy for combining them.
- [The 6 Types of Neuro-Symbolic Systems](https://harshakokel.com/posts/neurosymbolic-systems/) — **Harsha Kokel** — that taxonomy, one worked example per type.

## Books (free, with chapters)
- [*Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*](https://aima.cs.berkeley.edu/) — **Russell & Norvig** — one book that covers search, logic, probability, and learning; free sample chapters and code.
- [*Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction*](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book1.html) — **Kevin Murphy (2022)** — free PDF; the probabilistic view of everything else in this list.
